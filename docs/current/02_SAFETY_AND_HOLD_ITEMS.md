# Safety And HOLD Items

## Safety approach

Commands are organized by impact instead of arbitrary non-use. FLASH, frequency, transmit output, antenna switching, 8CH antenna auto-switch, UHF_SetInventoryParam, persistent settings, tag memory changes, Lock, and Kill require target device/ROM, parameter, effect, log, and recovery notes.

## HOLD items

- Formal external publication approval
- Human review of full PDF cross-checking
- Traceability completion for remaining field-level details
- License/IP policy finalization
- Individual real-device send confirmation for all 54 commands
- Overseas use or overseas sales
